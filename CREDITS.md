## Generated in code

Card faces, card backs, the roulette wheel, slot reel symbols (built-in pixel font) and cat meows
(synthesized) are all generated at runtime by the game's own code. The five non-black fur textures
are recolored from the Black Cat pack's texture by the scene builder.
